**Multi-Step State Probability via Marginalization**
An autonomous unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) patrols a contested maritime zone. At hourly intervals, its telemetry computer logs the status of its communications link as either **Online ($1$)** or **Offline ($0$)**. 

The transition matrix governing link status is:
$$T = \begin{pmatrix} P(X_{t+1}=0 \mid X_t=0) & P(X_{t+1}=1 \mid X_t=0) \\ P(X_{t+1}=0 \mid X_t=1) & P(X_{t+1}=1 \mid X_t=1) \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 0.60 & 0.40 \\ 0.10 & 0.90 \end{pmatrix}$$

At deployment ($t=0$), electronic warfare jamming knocks the link Offline with probability $1.0$ ($\mathbf{p}_0 = \langle P(X_0=0)=1.0, P(X_0=1)=0.0 \rangle$).

Compute the probability that the UAV communications link is successfully restored to **Online** at time $t=2$ ($P(X_2 = 1)$) by marginalizing over the intermediate state at $t=1$.

<details>
  <summary>Show solution</summary>

**Step 1:** Compute the state probability distribution at time $t=1$ ($\mathbf{p}_1$):
$$\mathbf{p}_1 = \mathbf{p}_0 T = \begin{pmatrix} 1.0 & 0.0 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} 0.60 & 0.40 \\ 0.10 & 0.90 \end{pmatrix}$$
$$P(X_1 = 0) = (1.0)(0.60) + (0.0)(0.10) = 0.60$$
$$P(X_1 = 1) = (1.0)(0.40) + (0.0)(0.90) = 0.40$$
$$\mathbf{p}_1 = \langle 0.60, 0.40 \rangle$$

**Step 2:** Compute $P(X_2 = 1)$ by marginalizing across both possible states at $t=1$:
$$P(X_2 = 1) = \sum_{i \in \{0, 1\}} P(X_1 = i) \cdot P(X_2 = 1 \mid X_1 = i)$$
$$P(X_2 = 1) = P(X_1 = 0) \cdot T_{01} + P(X_1 = 1) \cdot T_{11}$$
$$P(X_2 = 1) = (0.60 \times 0.40) + (0.40 \times 0.90)$$
$$P(X_2 = 1) = 0.2400 + 0.3600 = 0.6000$$

**Answer:**
$0.6000$ (or $60.00\%$). Two hours after deployment, the UAV has a 60% probability of having re-established an Online communications link.

</details>

**Joint Trajectory Evaluation**
Using the UAV communications Markov model from Exercise 1:

$T = \begin{pmatrix} 0.60 & 0.40 \\ 0.10 & 0.90 \end{pmatrix}$

Assume that at time $t=0$, the UAV begins with an Online connection: $P(X_0 = 1) = 1.0$.

Compute the joint probability of observing the specific temporal trajectory where the UAV drops offline at $t=1$, remains offline at $t=2$, and successfully reconnects at $t=3$:

$P(X_0 = 1, X_1 = 0, X_2 = 0, X_3 = 1)$

<details>
  <summary>Show solution</summary>

**Step 1:** Apply the Markov chain joint factorization rule:

$P(X_0=1, X_1=0, X_2=0, X_3=1) = P(X_0=1) \cdot P(X_1=0 \mid X_0=1) \cdot P(X_2=0 \mid X_1=0) \cdot P(X_3=1 \mid X_2=0)$

**Step 2:** Extract the individual transition probabilities from matrix $T$:
* $P(X_0 = 1) = 1.0$
* $P(X_1 = 0 \mid X_0 = 1) = T_{10} = 0.10$
* $P(X_2 = 0 \mid X_1 = 0) = T_{00} = 0.60$
* $P(X_3 = 1 \mid X_2 = 0) = T_{01} = 0.40$

**Step 3:** Calculate the product:
$P(X_0=1, X_1=0, X_2=0, X_3=1) = 1.0 \times 0.10 \times 0.60 \times 0.40 = 0.0240$

**Answer:**
$0.0240$ (or $2.40\%$).

</details>

**Stationary Distribution Computation**
A forward-deployed cyber defense sensor transitions between two operational threat postures:
* State 1: **Normal Patrol ($N$)**
* State 2: **Elevated Alert ($E$)**

The transition matrix between hours is:

$T = \begin{pmatrix} 0.85 & 0.15 \\ 0.35 & 0.65 \end{pmatrix}$

Where row 1 is Normal Patrol ($N$) and row 2 is Elevated Alert ($E$).

Calculate the long-run **Stationary Distribution** $\mathbf{\pi} = \langle \pi_N, \pi_E \rangle$ of this cyber system.

<details>
  <summary>Show solution</summary>

**Step 1:** Formulate the stationary distribution system of linear equations:
$$\mathbf{\pi} = \mathbf{\pi} T \quad \text{and} \quad \pi_N + \pi_E = 1.0$$
$$\begin{pmatrix} \pi_N & \pi_E \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} \pi_N & \pi_E \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} 0.85 & 0.15 \\ 0.35 & 0.65 \end{pmatrix}$$

**Step 2:** Expand the matrix multiplication for state $N$:

$\pi_N = 0.85 \pi_N + 0.35 \pi_E$

$0.15 \pi_N = 0.35 \pi_E \implies \pi_N = \frac{0.35}{0.15} \pi_E = \frac{7}{3} \pi_E$

**Step 3:** Substitute into the normalization condition:
$\frac{7}{3} \pi_E + \pi_E = 1.0$
$\frac{10}{3} \pi_E = 1.0 \implies \pi_E = \frac{3}{10} = 0.3000$

**Step 4:** Solve for $\pi_N$:
$\pi_N = 1.0 - 0.3000 = 0.7000$

**Answer:**
$\mathbf{\pi} = \langle \pi_N = 0.7000, \pi_E = 0.3000 \rangle$ (or $70\%$ Normal Patrol, $30\%$ Elevated Alert). In the long run, the system spends 70% of its operating lifetime in normal patrol and 30% in elevated alert, regardless of its starting state.

</details>
